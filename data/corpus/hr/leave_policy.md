title: Leave Policy
---
# Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; any further unused days expire on 31 March.

Leave of more than 2 days must be requested in Workday at least 5 working days in advance. Managers should respond to leave requests within 2 working days.

# Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

# Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks. Parental leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.
