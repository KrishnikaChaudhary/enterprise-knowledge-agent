# Salary bands

Level L3 salary band is $70,000 to $90,000. Level L4 salary band is $90,000 to $120,000. Level L5 salary band is $120,000 to $160,000.

# Bonus

The annual bonus target is 10% of base salary for L3 and L4, and 15% for L5. Bonuses are paid in March.
