---
title: Travel and Expense Policy
---
# Receipts and reimbursement

Receipts are required for every expense over $25. Expense claims must be submitted in Concur within 30 days of the expense, and approved claims are reimbursed within 14 days.

# Travel limits

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Hotels are capped at $220 per night in standard cities; ask your manager for pre-approval in high-cost cities.

Flights must be economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and needs VP approval before booking.

# Client entertainment

Client entertainment up to $100 per person needs manager approval. Anything above $100 per person needs approval from the department head.
