# Process

Misconduct complaints are acknowledged within 2 working days. An investigator who is independent of the reporting line is assigned, and the investigation should conclude within 30 days.

# Confidentiality

Only HR, Legal and the investigator may access case files. Retaliation against a person who raises a concern is itself a disciplinary offence.
