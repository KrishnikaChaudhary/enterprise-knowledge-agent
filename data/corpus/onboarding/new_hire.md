---
title: New Hire Onboarding Guide
---
# Before day one

Your laptop is provisioned and shipped or prepared before your first day. IT sends login details to your personal email the week before you start.

# First week

Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy for the first 4 weeks. Mandatory security training must be completed within 7 days of joining.

# First months

The probation period is 6 months. Benefits enrolment must be completed within 30 days of your start date, or you will be enrolled in the default plan.
