# Purchasing thresholds

Purchases over $5,000 need 3 competitive quotes. Purchases over $50,000 need written approval from the CFO.

# Vendors

Onboarding a new vendor takes about 10 business days, including security and legal review. Always check the preferred vendor list before contacting new suppliers.

# Software purchases

Any software that stores customer data needs a security review before purchase, regardless of price.
