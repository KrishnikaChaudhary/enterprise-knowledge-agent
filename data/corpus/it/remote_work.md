---
title: Hybrid and Remote Work Policy
---
# Office attendance

Teams follow a hybrid model: employees work from the office 3 days per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time.

# Home office support

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $400. The company also pays $40 per month toward home internet.

# Working abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year with manager approval. Longer stays need approval from HR and Legal because of tax and visa rules.
