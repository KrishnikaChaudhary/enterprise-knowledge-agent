# Accounts and devices

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; change one immediately if you suspect it is compromised.

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Use the approved file-sharing service instead.

# Public networks

You must use the company VPN whenever you are on public Wi-Fi, such as a cafe, airport or hotel network.

# Reporting incidents

Report a lost or stolen laptop to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of noticing. Report suspicious emails with the Report Phishing button in your mail client. Do not forward phishing emails to colleagues.
