---
# Priorities and response times

Priority 1 (service down) issues get a first response within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day, every day. Priority 2 (major feature degraded) issues get a response within 2 hours during business hours. Priority 3 (minor issues and questions) get a response within 1 business day.

# Escalation

If a Priority 1 ticket has no response after 30 minutes, it escalates automatically to the duty manager.

# Service credits

When monthly uptime falls below the contracted SLA, the customer receives a 10% service credit for that month. Credits must be requested within 30 days.
